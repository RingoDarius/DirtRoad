Dirt Road

Formed in a basement in South Ogden, we’re a high-energy cover band blending hard rock grit with country soul. What started as a few musicians jamming together has grown into a full live show built around crowd favorites from artists like ZZ Top, 3 Doors Down, and Scorpions.

We focus on tight musicianship, strong vocals, and creating a live experience that gets people singing along and sticking around. Whether it’s an outdoor stage or a local venue, we bring the same energy every time.
